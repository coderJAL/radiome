The Station

Built for the Malayali listener

Radio Me ♪ ♫ is a free 24/7 online radio station dedicated to delivering a high-quality audio experience through an innovative 5.1 digital surrounding system.

Broadcasting from Puthanathani, Malappuram District, Kerala, we carry a huge collection of hit songs — mostly streamed on listener request.

  • Instant playback — no tuning, no buffering drama
  • Listener-request driven playlist all day long
  • Curated day-parts from morning vibe to dark tones
  • Podcasts on culture, tech, health & everyday life
